Digital overlays on photographs of manuscript pages

This app recolors a photo you already have so faint marks are easier to look at. Advisory visualization. Not a lab spectrometer, not real UV photography hardware, not a forensic proof of hidden ink, not OCR, not a claim of scribal truth. Synthetic UV is a 365–400 nm look from an ordinary photo. Balance never invents marks. The human still reads the page.

Overlays are not forensic proof. They reweight pixels in a photograph you already have.
