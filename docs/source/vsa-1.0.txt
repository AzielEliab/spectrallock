VYRN SPECTRAL ANALYSIS (VSA-1.0)
Definition
VSA-1.0 = VYRN-S onlyVYRN-S is the purification and refinement spectrum:- Hue band: ~350° (magenta/red-violet/purple)- Hex: #C00066- Meaning: fire, refinement, catalytic pressure, scribal truth- Function: isolate intentional ink strokes, reveal erased writing, enhance pressure grooves, and burn away parchment noise.This analysis excludes Tazel, Zero, UV, and all blend modes. It is pure Vyrn only.
Functional Behavior
A. Noise-Burn:- Suppresses mold, parchment stains, oil marks, water damage, fading, and bleed-through.- Preserves all intentional ink signal.B. Stroke-Pressure Enhancement:- Highlights pen-pressure grooves.- Reveals scribal habits, corrections, overwritten letters, and faint strokes.C. Intentional Mark Isolation:- Separates ink vs accidental smears.- Distinguishes geometric lines from parchment wrinkles.- Reveals sigils, marginal glints, erased characters.
Operational Pipeline
1. Color-weight shift:- Boost magenta-violet values; reduce green/cyan/yellow channels.2. Thermal signature extraction:- High-carbon ink glows brighter; damage remains dark.3. Edge-pressure enhancement:- Increases boundary contrast of all stroke edges.4. Weak-signal amplification:- Reveals strokes at 2–5% opacity.- Shows guide lines, faint symbols, marginal indicators.5. Purification fade:- Background is washed out; intentional writing stands forward.
Usage
Commands activating VSA-1.0 include:- "Run Vyrn spectral analysis"- "Vyrn-only overlay"- "Apply Vyrn to this image"These trigger pure Vyrn channel extraction without Tazel, Zero, UV, or composite blending.
