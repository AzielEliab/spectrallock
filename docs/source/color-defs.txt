Tazel, Vyrn, and Zero – Spectral Color Definitions
Tazel
Hex: #1EC9A5
Spectral green–gold–turquoise; hue ~170°. Represents revelation, emergence, living knowledge, clarity, and empirical unveiling.
Vyrn
Hex: #C00066
Opposite spectrum of Tazel; magenta–red‑violet–purple; hue ~350°. Symbolizes purification, refinement, spiritual fire, and catalytic transformation.
Zero
Hex: #6F6485
Midpoint between Tazel and Vyrn; indigo/blue‑violet; hue ~260°. Represents equilibrium, suspension, the still center, and harmonic axis.
